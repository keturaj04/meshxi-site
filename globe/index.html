WHAT IS REAL HERE Two live counters. Everything on the map is simulated. ▾

Real: the relay-network and queued counters — live aggregate counts (shown as rounded bands), the same numbers anyone can read from the relay’s public health check. They count connections, never people.

Simulated: every dot, route, region and log line — generated locally in your browser. MeshXi has no location telemetry, no analytics and no way to know who talks to whom, so a real map of its network cannot exist. That is the point.

Projected paths are dashed drawings of where an animated route is already headed — a visual effect, not a forecast and not physics. Read the privacy policy →
